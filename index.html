03 · Aurora outlook

Aurora watch

Aurora forecasts come from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center and the UAF Geophysical Institute, but clear skies matter just as much, so check local cloud cover too. Follow @todayinak for Alaska events and aurora tips.

Check the live forecast

UAF Geophysical Institute aurora forecast →NOAA 30-minute aurora forecast →

Forecasts change fast. Look at them again right before you head out.

Aurora season in Anchorage runs roughly mid-August to April, best September to March. Look for clear, dark skies; the best hours are often around midnight (about 10 PM–3 AM). Sightings are never guaranteed, so plan for a few nights.

Where to look

  • Glen AlpsAnchorage Hillside, Chugach State ParkUp above the city lights with a wide view north over town.
  • Point WoronzofWest Anchorage, near the airportOpen view across Cook Inlet, a few minutes from downtown.
  • EklutnaNorth on the Glenn Hwy (Eklutna Tailrace & Knik River Valley)Darker skies, well away from Anchorage's glow.

Before you go

  • Look north, away from city lights.
  • Dress in warm layers.
  • Park only in real lots, never on a highway shoulder.
  • Clear, moonless nights are best. A bright moon washes out faint auroras.
