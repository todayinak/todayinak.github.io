Today in AK is a free guide to events, food trucks and things to do around Anchorage, Mat-Su, Eagle River and Girdwood. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it. We try to collect as little as we can.

What this site is

A static website, hosted on GitHub Pages. There is no account to create. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visitor counter, to see which pages are read and which tour, stay or ticket links are clicked. It does not use cookies or identify you. See GoatCounter's privacy page.

What stays on your device

Your browser can remember a few choices on this device only, using the browser's own local storage. We do not set our own cookies today. Those saved choices are:

  • The color theme you pick (Aurora is the default)
  • Events you heart, so they are easy to find again
  • The region you last looked at

We do not receive any of that. Clearing your browser data removes it. In a private window, it may not be saved at all.

Some guide pages include affiliate links. Those partners may set their own cookies when you follow a link. We say so near those links. See our affiliate disclosure.

Email and event forms

If you sign up for This weekend in AK, we use the email address you give us to send that weekly email. We will not sell your address.

Newsletter sign-ups are handled by Beehiiv. When you submit the form, Beehiiv receives your email address so we can send the weekly email. You can unsubscribe from the email itself, or write to todayinak@gmail.com and we will take you off the list. You can also sign up on our newsletter page at todays-newsletter-29fbf8.beehiiv.com. Beehiiv's own practices are described in Beehiiv's privacy policy.

If you submit an event, we receive what you put in the form: your name and email, the event name, date and time, venue, details, a link, and whether you want a free listing or a Featured listing. We use that to add the event to the guide and, if you asked for Featured, to follow up about payment. Until a form service is connected, this also goes to todayinak@gmail.com.

If you pay for a Featured or Featured Plus listing, payments are processed by Stripe. Checkout also asks for the event name and date so we can match your payment to your submission. We do not see your full card number. Stripe's own practices are described in Stripe's privacy policy.

You can email todayinak@gmail.com to ask us to delete an email address or an event submission we still have.

Text alerts

If you join the text-alert waitlist, we collect the mobile number you give us, and your first name if you share it. We use this only for Today in AK alerts: first to tell you when texts launch, and after that, if you stay on the list, to send those alerts. We will not sell your number, and we will not share it with third parties for their marketing.

No texts are sent until alerts launch. When they do, we will text you once to let you know. After that, you can text STOP to leave the list, or HELP for help. You can also email todayinak@gmail.com and we will take you off the list.

Message and data rates may apply. These texts will be about Today in AK events and related updates only.

Weather and aurora on the home page

The home page asks two public services for the Anchorage forecast, so we can show the current temperature and a simple aurora reading:

  • Open-Meteo, for the Anchorage temperature
  • NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center, for the planetary Kp index

Those requests go from your browser to those services. We ask for Anchorage, not your location. They may see standard request information, such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies.

Hosting and search

Because the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and the time) as part of running the service, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not get a live feed of who is visiting.

We also use search tools, such as Google Search Console, to see how the site shows up in search. Those tools use aggregate reports. They are not a tracking script in the pages.

The typefaces on this site, Montserrat and Inter, are hosted with the site. We do not load fonts from Google or another font service.

Links to other sites

Event pages, ticket buttons, Instagram and Facebook take you off our site. Those sites have their own privacy practices. Affiliate tour and stay links may also cause a partner to set cookies when you follow them, and we note that near the links. See our affiliate disclosure.

Children

This is a public events guide. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child sent us an email, write to todayinak@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If this page changes, we will update the date at the top.
