      Town Square is fenced this winter

      Town Square Park is fenced for construction this winter, so the tree lighting moved to 4th Avenue on Nov 28. More about the park work is on the Municipality's Town Square project page.

      Also watch for

      • Eagle River tree lighting. Usually at Eagle River Town Square Park, as part of the Chugiak-Eagle River Chamber's Winter Wonderland Celebration. We'll add a pin when the 2026 date is posted.
      • Wasilla tree lighting. Usually at Iditapark. We'll add a pin when the 2026 date is posted.
      • Star on the Mountain. The Palmer star usually comes on in late November and stays lit until the last Iditarod musher finishes. We'll add the switch-on date when it's posted.

      Tips for going out

      • Go after dark, and dress for standing outside at the walk-throughs. Zoo Lights and the Botanical Garden have set hours, and last entry at the Garden is 30 minutes before close.
      • Buy Zoo Lights and Brighter Winter Nights tickets ahead when you can. Both are closed on Christmas Day. The Garden is also closed on New Year's Day.
      • At Bright Up the Night, stay in the car. In neighborhoods, don't block driveways or park in front of houses.
      • Check 511.alaska.gov before you drive to Palmer or Girdwood.

      For markets, Nutcracker and the rest of the season, see holiday events and the December list.

      Questions

      Is the Town Square tree lighting happening this year?

      Yes, but not in Town Square Park. The park is fenced for reconstruction this winter, and new park lighting may not go in until spring. The Anchorage Downtown Partnership moved the ceremony to 4th Avenue, between E and F Streets, in front of Historic City Hall. It's Saturday, November 28, 2026, from 6 to 8 PM, with the tree lighting at 6:15 PM.

      When does Zoo Lights start?

      Opening weekend is November 27–29, 2026, from 5 to 8 PM, then it continues on scheduled nights through February 28, 2027. The zoo is closed on Christmas Day. Check the Alaska Zoo page for the full calendar and tickets.

      Is there a drive-through lights display near Anchorage?

      Yes. Bright Up the Night at the Alaska State Fairgrounds in Palmer runs November 26, 2026 through January 2, 2027. Early dates are Thursday–Sunday only; from December 10 it is nightly, 5 to 9 PM. Stay in your vehicle. The Fair's Yuletide Festival is not happening in 2026.

      Can I add my house to the map?

      Yes. Use the form on this page — it takes about 30 seconds. You'll drop a pin or use your location, pick a display type, and confirm you live there or have the owner's OK. We look at every pin before it goes on the map, and we never show your name.

      Will my exact address show up?

      Not unless you ask it to. The default is a street- or block-level pin, like “near the 3400 block of X St.” You can opt in to an exact pin if you want people to find the precise spot.

      How do I change or take down a listing?

      If you left an email, we'll send a private link you can use to edit or remove the pin. You can also write to todayinak@gmail.com and we'll pull it on the next update.

      Are neighborhood houses on the map now?

      Official events and landmarks are on the map now. Home displays go up after people send them in and we look them over.
