Today in AK is a free guide to things to do in Alaska. This page explains how we may earn a little money from some links, and how we will tell you when that is happening.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. That means we may earn a small commission if you buy tickets, book a tour or reserve a stay through them. It does not cost you extra.

Some guide pages include affiliate links to tours and stays. Ticket links on event listings still go straight to the venue, organizer or ticket office. When a link is an affiliate link, we put a short note near it:

Some links may earn Today in AK a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Eventbrite

If we take part in Eventbrite's affiliate program, this also applies, in the wording they ask affiliates to use:

As an Eventbrite Affiliate, I earn from qualifying ticket purchases and paid events published on Eventbrite.

Other partners we may work with

We may also use programs from companies such as Viator, GetYourGuide, Stay22 and Expedia. Each company has its own terms. A booking or ticket purchase is between you and that company, not with Today in AK.

Featured listings

Some events on the site are paid placements. Those are marked as Featured, and they are advertising, separate from affiliate links. You can read more, or submit an event, on the submit your event page.
