Anchorage nightlife questions

What is there to do in Anchorage at night?

Anchorage nightlife centers on live music, comedy, trivia, karaoke and brewery events in Spenard, downtown and Midtown. This page lists the week’s events in one place and links to separate pages for comedy, live music and trivia.

Do I need ID?

Most of these events are marked 21+, and venues check ID at the door. Some bars and breweries welcome all ages earlier in the day, so confirm the venue’s age policy first.

How often is this updated?

Every Thursday, using the same checked event list as the rest of Today in AK. Add your own event with the submit form.

How we pick what’s listed

We list events marked 21+ or 18+, plus music, comedy, trivia, karaoke, bingo and tasting nights at bars and breweries. We keep the list to bar, brewery and venue nights, and we skip bar crawls, political events and anything outside that scope. Listings are not an endorsement, and venue age rules always win.
