Where can I see comedy in Anchorage?

Stand-up, improv and open mics happen at bars, theaters and taprooms around Anchorage, Spenard and Eagle River. The list above shows what is coming up this week and next.

Are comedy shows 21+?

Many are 21+ or 18+, and the age appears on each listing. Some improv shows are all ages, so check the venue.

How we pick what’s listed

We list events marked 21+ or 18+, plus music, comedy, trivia, karaoke, bingo and tasting nights at bars and breweries. We keep the list to bar, brewery and venue nights, and we skip bar crawls, political events and anything outside that scope. Listings are not an endorsement, and venue age rules always win.
