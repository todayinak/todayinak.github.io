Anchorage Public Library

ClosedLibraries

All locations, including Loussac, Eagle River, Girdwood and the branches

Closed Thursday, Nov. 26, and Friday, Nov. 27.

Every Anchorage Public Library location is closed both days, including Loussac, Chugiak-Eagle River, Mountain View, Muldoon, Gerrish (Girdwood) and the Mobile Library.

Details

Anchorage Parks and Recreation

ClosedRec centers

Rec centers, pools and park offices

Rec centers, pools, chalets and park offices are closed Nov. 26 and Nov. 27. The greenhouse is open 8 a.m.–3:30 p.m. both days.

Closed sites include Spenard Recreation, Fairview Recreation, Mountain View Community Center, West, Dimond, Bartlett and East pools, Kincaid Chalet, Lidia Selkregg Chalet, and the main and permit offices. From the official 2026 facility holiday hours.

Details

Anchorage trash and recycling

OpenTrash pickup

Solid Waste Services collection area

Curbside garbage, recycling and composting still run on Thanksgiving Day. Transfer stations, the landfill and the materials recovery facility close at 2 p.m. The SWS office is closed.

On Friday, Nov. 27, curbside collections and facilities are open, and the office stays closed. From the official SWS 2026 holiday calendar. Private haulers outside the SWS area may differ.
